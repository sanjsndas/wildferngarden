Cookies Policy

🍪 Last updated: 2 September 2026 🌱 Wildfern Garden House

We keep it simple. At Wildfern Garden House, we use minimal, essential cookies to make our website work properly. We do not use tracking, advertising, or third-party profiling cookies. Your privacy is our priority.

📌 In brief: We only use cookies that are strictly necessary for the operation of the site. No analytics, no ads, no social trackers. Just a functional, tranquil experience.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember your preferences and improve performance. We use only the bare minimum to ensure a smooth visit.

2. Cookies we use

We use the following strictly necessary cookies:

  • Session cookie: A temporary cookie that expires when you close your browser. It helps our server recognize your session so you can navigate the site without interruption.
  • Cookie preference: A small cookie that remembers your choice about our cookie policy so we don't show the banner repeatedly.

We do not use any analytics, advertising, or social media cookies. No data is shared with third parties for tracking purposes.

3. Your choices

Because we only use essential cookies, you do not need to provide consent for non-essential tracking. However, you can still manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Please note that disabling essential cookies may affect the functionality of the site.

4. Third-party services

We do not integrate third-party services (like Google Analytics, ad networks, or social plugins) that place cookies on your device. Our site is self-contained and privacy-focused.

5. Updates to this policy

If we ever change our cookie practices, we will update this page clearly. Any changes will be effective immediately upon posting. We encourage you to check back occasionally.

🍃 Still have questions? If you'd like more details about our cookie usage, please contact us — we’re happy to help.
