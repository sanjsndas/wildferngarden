Privacy Policy

📜 Last updated: 2 September 2026 🌱 Wildfern Garden House

Your privacy matters to us. At Wildfern Garden House, we are committed to protecting your personal data and being transparent about how we use it. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

📌 In brief: We collect minimal data to improve your experience. We never sell or share your information with third parties for marketing. We use essential cookies only.

1. Information we collect

We collect only the data that helps us understand how our readers engage with our content and to provide a better service. This includes:

  • Usage data: Pages visited, time spent, and referral sources (via anonymized analytics).
  • Contact information: If you reach out via email or our contact form, we retain your name and email address solely to respond.
  • Newsletter sign-ups: Email address and preferences (only if you opt in).

2. How we use your information

We use your data to:

  • Improve our website and content based on reader interests.
  • Respond to inquiries and comments.
  • Send occasional updates about new articles, events, or resources (only with your consent).

3. Cookies & tracking

We use only essential cookies to ensure the website functions properly. We do not use any tracking cookies for advertising or third-party profiling. For more details, please see our Cookies Policy.

4. Data security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. All data is stored securely and access is restricted to essential personnel.

5. Third-party services

We may use trusted third-party services (e.g., hosting providers, email platforms) that process data on our behalf. These providers are contractually obligated to keep your information confidential and secure.

6. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction or deletion of your data.
  • Withdraw consent for communications at any time.
  • Lodge a complaint with a data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us.

7. Children’s privacy

Wildfern Garden House is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

✉️ Questions? If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please get in touch — we’re happy to clarify.
