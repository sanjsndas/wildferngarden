📅 Our journey

2024

Seeds of an idea

A small group of gardeners started sharing field notes in a private forum. The conversations grew into a publication.

2025

First field guide

We published our first seasonal guide, “Rewilding the Border,” which resonated with gardeners across climates.

2026

Wildfern Garden House

We launched this digital field journal to share practical, beautiful, and resilient gardening knowledge with the world.
